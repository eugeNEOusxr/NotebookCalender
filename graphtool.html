📈 Graphing Tool — plot points & connect them

Closed / Open dot — tap the grid to drop a point (● included · ○ not included).
Segment · Ray · Line — tap two grid points. A ray extends past the 2nd point; a line extends both ways.
Drop an open dot on a line's endpoint to mark "doesn't touch."
Drag any dot or line to move it — drag it off the grid to delete it.
